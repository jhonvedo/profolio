With an autocomplete assistant, the unit of work is the line or the block: you type, the model suggests, you accept with Tab. With Claude, the unit of work is the task: you describe an outcome, Claude explores the repository, plans, edits several files, runs the build and tests, and hands you a reviewable change.

That changes what an expert developer is good at: less typing and more specifying, giving context, setting limits and reviewing. The whole program revolves around those four skills.

Explore→Plan→Implement→Verify→Commit / PR

How to know it worked

Take a baseline before session 1 and repeat the measurement a month after session 6:

  • Adoption: active people and sessions per week in the plan's Claude Code analytics dashboard.
  • Flow: time from issue to PR and review rounds per PR.
  • Quality: reverted PRs and bugs that come from changes made with Claude.
  • Individual use: /insights generates a report on each person's recent sessions. Have everyone share one pattern they want to fix with the team.
