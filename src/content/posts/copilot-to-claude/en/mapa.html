With Copilot, the unit of work is the line or the block: you type, the model suggests, you accept with Tab. With Claude, the unit of work is the task: you describe an outcome, Claude explores the repository, plans, edits several files, runs the build and tests, and hands you a reviewable change.

That changes what an expert developer is good at: less typing and more specifying, giving context, setting limits and reviewing. The whole program revolves around those four skills.

Explore→Plan→Implement→Verify→Commit / PR
